About Me!

Hello! Welcome to my Website! I am Francis. I'm an aspiring
freelance Artist and Illustrator based in Philippines.

I love learning new things in art to better understand
what my clients want. So far I've tried Live2D, making
a game on my own, Graphic design, and I'm getting ready
to start a webcomic!

Art

Character Designs, Full Illustrations, and Storyboard Art.

I mainly focus on drawing characters and backgrounds in a simple anime style. Currently, I'm practicing textures and composition through League of Legend's splash arts and
Arknights character designs. My inspirations include zePocketDroid, Avogado6, and Saito Naoki.

I mainly use Adobe Photoshop for all the 2D workloads, and Blender to do greybox and compositions.

Other Experiences

I've got hands-on experience with Live2D, from creating art to rigging models during my downtime. I also have a background in Computer Programming and UX Design.

Currently learning in the spectrum of game development using Godot engine, unity, and GameMaker.

On the side note, I'm planning start a short webcomic series.
